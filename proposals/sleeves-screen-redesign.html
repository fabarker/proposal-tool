What's hard today

The Sleeves tab at 1600px, on the first sleeve of Investment Grade Fixed Income under PMG Multi-Asset Portfolio. The numbered points are explained below.

1 2 3 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 10

Live console, 1 October 2026. Nothing was saved while taking it.

  • 1The three panes barely separate. Categories, the sleeve list and the editor are divided by 1px lines in #EEF1F4 on white, so the screen reads as one sheet of text, not three areas with three jobs.
  • 2The implementation type is easy to miss. It decides everything in the list, yet it is four 10px pills wrapping onto two lines inside the middle pane, looking like tags rather than the screen's main filter.
  • 3Two buttons create a sleeve. "+ New sleeve" in the list header and a large green "+ Create sleeve" in the footer do the same thing, and the green one is the loudest control on the screen.
  • 4The sleeve has no heading. Its name only appears inside a text box; where it lives (category · type) is 11px grey, and "+ Add edition" and "Archive this sleeve…" are tiny buttons beside it.
  • 5It is always a form. Opening a sleeve to read it drops you straight into editable fields, including a greyed Category box that repeats the left pane. There is no calm "this is what the sleeve holds" view.
  • 6Editions dominate. An advanced feature (none of the 101 live sleeves uses one) takes a paragraph of grey help and a full-width "+ Add a rule" bar above the products, which are what you came to see.
  • 7Product facts are tiny. Each product's asset class, vehicle, source and fee group is a 10px grey line starting "— ·", and cost, liquidity and minimum are not shown at all.
  • 8The list repeats noise. Every row says "created 2 Sept 2026, 19:17 by francisbarker" - identical for all 101 - while what differs (status, what it holds) is not there.
  • 9Save is far from the work. It sits in the footer's far corner, pale blue whether or not anything has changed, and nothing on screen says "you have unsaved changes" until you try to leave.
  • 10The footer speaks to developers. "Library · sleeves.db · 101 sleeves · 107 versions on record" names a database file. History is a collapsed row at the very bottom of a scrolling pane.

At 1280px, scrolled to the foot of the editor: History is the last row, under the provenance line in 10px grey.

What every proposal does differently

Visible areasPanels have real borders (#AEBAC7), titled headers on a tinted band, and space between them.
The type is the main filterA full-size segmented control or selector that says what the list is showing.
A heading per sleeveThe sleeve's name as a title, where it lives in words, and status chips that say what they mean.
Read, then editMost proposals open a sleeve read-only with one clear Edit; while editing, a tinted bar says so and holds Save.
Products firstWhat the sleeve holds, with weights, a total and the product facts in readable type. Editions are kept but sit lower.
One way to createA single "New sleeve" action, placed where its category is in hand.

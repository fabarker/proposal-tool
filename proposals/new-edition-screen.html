Ten ways to make a new edition clearer

The screen for adding an edition to a sleeve: a version of the sleeve that particular strategic portfolios get instead of the fallback.   • 1The entry point hides the idea. “Who gets it” explains editions in one long sentence, and the button is the only way in. Nothing says which portfolios currently get which version.
  • 2It opens as the whole sleeve editor. Sleeve name and category are greyed boxes, the note is editable, and the page reads like editing the sleeve rather than adding a version of it.
  • 3The label invites the wrong thing. Its placeholder says “Leave blank for the fallback”, but this sleeve already has its fallback, so blank can never be saved here.
  • 4Before any rule it says the wrong thing. “The fallback: applies wherever no other edition of this name does” describes the sleeve you started from, not the edition you are making.
  • 5Products are copied silently. The fallback's products are already in the table; the only hint is a 10px line at the foot (“starts as a copy of the fallback”). Changes are not marked against it.
  • 6Create edition never says what it creates, and “Unsaved changes” appears as soon as a label is typed, while the rule problems come one at a time in the bar.
  • 7The rule logic is unexplained. Ticking two values in a row means “either”, ticking in two rows means “both”, and a second rule means “or”: only a grey sentence and a small “or” say so. The values are capitalised codes (CONSMOD, MODAGG, NA).
  • 8A count, not the portfolios. “43 of 172” does not say which 43, how many stay on the fallback, or that both real-assets variants of a portfolio are counted.
  • 9Clashes read as raw keys (“GBP|Moderate|Full|0, …”) and are cut off after four, so you cannot see the whole overlap you need to remove.
  • 10The block repeats in two places with different words: the bar says “narrow them”, the line under the rules says “Narrow one of them”, and Create is disabled with no pointer to what to change.

What an edition is (as every option explains it)

A sleeve can have several editions. PWAs always pick the sleeve by its name. Which edition they get depends on the strategic portfolio they are implementing, and they never see the edition's label.
The fallback is the edition with no rules: every portfolio that no other edition claims gets it. A sleeve has exactly one.
Rules say which portfolios. Within a rule, values in the same field mean either (GBP or EUR) and different fields mean both (GBP and Full). Several rules mean or. Hedging and the real-assets exclusion are not rule fields, so both variants of a portfolio go together.
One edition per portfolio. Two labelled editions may not claim the same portfolio: the overlap is refused, with the portfolios named. An edition lives under one implementation type, in its sleeve's category.

What every option keeps

  • Start from “+ Add an edition” on the sleeve page, or the right-click menu
  • The sleeve's name, category and implementation type fixed and shown, not editable
  • A label, required, unique among the sleeve's editions (case and spacing ignored)
  • One or more rules over currency, risk level and allocation; an empty rule refused
  • Live count against the 172 strategic portfolios, and which ones
  • Who keeps the fallback, and who keeps each other edition
  • Overlaps with other editions shown with the portfolios named, and blocking Create
  • Products and weights: start from the fallback's (marked changes) or blank
  • Add a product from the catalogue dialog; weights clean numbers that make exactly 100%
  • Optional note
  • Create that says what it creates; problems shown next to what they are about
  • Cancel asks before discarding; afterwards you land on the new edition
